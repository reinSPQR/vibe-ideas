## Per-idea notes

1. **Terraforming Mars Orbital Production Dial Tower** — Demand verified: TFM is confirmed BGG top-10 (rank ~8 at time of check), a large and active heavy-euro owner base. Search for the mechanism itself turned up only static cube holders/bins (Thingiverse thing:2338058, MakerWorld bins), no rotating dial, so differentiation holds but only earns mid-range credit since "rotating dial replacing a cube track" is a very common accessory pattern across many games, not a unique angle to this title. Margin and producibility are solid but capped short of top marks by the 6x repeated detent joint, even though it's well-justified with a generous 30mm diameter.

2. **Catan Probability Setup Rack & Claim Tracker** — Highest demand score in the set: Catan's 32-45M copies sold is independently confirmed (NPR, publisher site), giving it the largest verifiable owner base of any idea this turn. Differentiation is weaker than the rationale claims, though: search turned up existing "Largest Army/Longest Road" physical trophy-token replacements (Printables model 6762, thing:6207027) that already solve the "who currently holds this" tracking problem the claim-tracker half addresses — the idea's claim was scoped to the *combination* (probability tray + tracker), which is true and survives search, but the tracker half alone is not the gap it might read as. Producibility is clean (3 parts, 2 generous low-load pivots) and margin is well-supported by a cited real Catan-accessory price band.

3. **Splendor Reserve Privacy Shield & Noble Display Stand** — Splendor's 2014 Spiel des Jahres nomination is confirmed, and it has genuine casual/gateway crossover reach, though a smaller and older audience than Catan/Monopoly, capping demand below the top tier. Search for "reserved card privacy shield" returned only generic chip/card holders and organizer inserts with no privacy-blocking mechanism, and the search summary explicitly flagged no privacy-shield variant found — differentiation claim holds. Best producibility profile in the set: 1-2 parts, single living hinge, one explicit critical tolerance (hinge thickness), matching the lowest-risk template from prior turns.

4. **Chess In-Play Captured Piece Value Tray** — Demand check surfaced strong, current, well-sourced numbers (Chess.com's 200M+ registered users, the confirmed Queen's Gambit signup spike), but per the Turn 3 lesson on verified-fact-vs-verified-argument, online chess platform growth is an inferential link to over-the-board physical accessory purchases, not a direct one — docked accordingly rather than fully credited. Differentiation holds (search found only full sets and storage boxes, plus one incidental forum comment about a magnetic lid, not a dedicated in-play tray). Cleanest producibility profile of the whole batch: 2 flat, jointless, hardware-free parts.

5. **Ticket to Ride Destination Completion Flip Clip** — TTR's 2004 Spiel des Jahres win and Days of Wonder's continued map-expansion releases are confirmed, supporting a genuinely large, active owner base. Search found only card holders and box inserts, no completion-status indicator, so the narrow claim survives. Producibility is the weak point: 12 identical parts each needing a small (0.6mm) bistable flex hinge to survive 50+ flexes per game across repeated play, plus a two-color print requirement (with single-color fallback) — a real, not-fully-mitigated fatigue and color-registration risk that the notes acknowledge but don't fully resolve.

6. **Pandemic Outbreak Dial Cube Tray** — The rationale's central demand citation ("well over 8 million units sold") does not hold up: the only verifiable figure (Z-Man Games' own 2021 announcement) is 5 million, meaning the claim overstates the checkable number by 60%+. Per the rubric's treatment of unverifiable/inflated demand claims, this caps the category near the floor despite Pandemic otherwise being a legitimately strong, top-100 BGG co-op title. Differentiation and producibility are both solid on their own merits (existing cube organizers were found, but none combine an outbreak-track dial into the lid; 2 parts, 1 generous-diameter detent joint).

7. **Root Faction Reputation & Dominance Stand** — Root's ~BGG rank 30 and its real, ongoing expansion line (Riverfolk, Underworld, Marauder, Clockwork) are confirmed, supporting a genuine, if niche, dedicated-fanbase demand case. Search explicitly returned no per-faction rotating score dial for Root, so differentiation holds. Margin is the weak spot: a 5-hour print for a $28 four-pack repeats the exact anti-pattern flagged in Turn 3 — pricing justified via the fanbase's general upgrade-accessory spend rather than an explicit print-time-to-price stress test for this specific object.

8. **Monopoly Banker Station: Cash Tray & Deed Carousel** — Monopoly's 275M-copy Guinness record is confirmed, the single strongest demand fact in the batch, and search explicitly confirmed no rotating deed carousel exists among current banker-tray designs — both top-tier scores. Margin is undercut by the same Turn-3-flagged pattern as idea 7: a 5-hour, 150g print sold at $26 is justified only by category-level Etsy pricing, with no explicit time/price arithmetic for this specific 8-part assembly.

9. **Kingdom Card Mechanical Randomizer Wheel** — Dominion's 2009 Spiel des Jahres win and its real, recent Rising Sun expansion (Rio Grande Games, confirmed via multiple retailers) support genuine ongoing demand, though the audience (deckbuilding-genre hobbyists) is narrower than the mass-market titles in this set. Differentiation is the strongest part of this idea: search results were unambiguous that every existing Dominion randomizer is software (apps, GitHub repos, websites), with no physical mechanical competitor found. Margin repeats the same unaddressed 5-hour-print-vs-price pattern as ideas 7 and 8. Producibility is the lowest in the set: a large-diameter printed axle-in-housing bearing with no hardware insert, plus a thin (1mm) spring pawl expected to survive repeated engagement against 40 notches — exactly the "printed mechanism replacing hardware" risk flagged back in Turn 1, still not mitigated with a hardware insert or explicit safety-margin justification.

10. **Playmat Wind-Lock Corner Clip Set** — The MTG/TCG market itself is confirmed enormous, but the specific demand evidence cited is a search engine's own hedge ("may still be a niche product opportunity") rather than any quantified signal that outdoor/convention wind-slippage is an actual purchase driver — this is treated as inconclusive evidence per the rubric and capped well below the ceiling rather than credited as support. Differentiation holds cleanly (only storage tubes/cases found, no in-use wind/edge accessory). Producibility and margin are both excellent: 4 identical, jointless, hardware-free small parts with a fast, cheap print — the template this pipeline should keep defaulting to when demand can actually be substantiated.

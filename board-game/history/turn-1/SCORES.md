## Per-idea notes

1. **Settler-Style Resource Bank Tray with Dials** — Verified: "Catan organizer" is a genuinely large, sustained Etsy/Printables search category (multiple dedicated Etsy market pages: catan_organizer, catan_organizer_3d_print, catan_piece_holder), confirming real accessory demand for this specific title, not just novelty. Differentiation is real but not maximal — most listings found were flat trays/piece holders, and I found no exact match combining resource storage with rotating longest-road/largest-army dials, but the underlying "Catan organizer" space itself is extremely crowded so the bar for standout was high. Small part count, single-color-viable, tight tolerances — the strongest producibility prompt of the set. This is the model to repeat: named-game + verified-searched pain point + a genuinely missing specific feature.

2. **Birdhouse Nest Sorter for Wingspan** — Verified: Wingspan has a well-documented, active aftermarket upgrade market (MeepleSource, Top Shelf Gamer, BGG accessory listings for egg/food/bird upgrades), so the demand claim holds up better than most. Differentiation capped moderately because felt/sculpted "nest" holders for eggs already exist as a product concept in this exact fanbase (just not birdhouse-shaped or roof-lidded) — the core "eggs go in a nest container" idea isn't new, only the birdhouse packaging and closable roof are. Solid, low-cost, well-specified prompt.

3. **Catapult-Trigger Castle Dice Tower** — Demand for kinetic/catapult dice towers is real and confirmed (Etsy has multiple established "dice catapult" listings: HexCat, Pachinko Dice Tower w/ Catapult, Mechanical Dice Tower), but that same search also surfaced near-identical existing products — this idea is not solving an underserved niche, it's entering a category with direct, named competitors already selling the exact "catapult flings dice down a tower" mechanic. Differentiation capped hard at 5/15 for that reason. Producibility is the bigger problem: a 0.8mm-thick printed torsion flexure as the sole spring mechanism (no metal hardware) is exactly the "fragile feature needing manual judgment to get right" the rubric warns about — this needed either a metal spring/elastic band call-out or a much larger safety margin on the flexure thickness.

4. **Cribbage Peg-Free Dial Scorekeeper** — The rationale's core hook — a TikTok/Instagram-driven cribbage resurgence among younger players — could not be verified; a direct search for this returned no evidence of cribbage trending on TikTok in 2025/2026. Per rubric this caps demand at 25/55, and I scored below that cap given cribbage is also a genuinely niche, aging-demographic game regardless of the trend claim. The lost-peg complaint itself is plausible but wasn't independently confirmed either. Differentiation is actually good — no existing printed physical dial-based no-peg board was found (only apps, and one historical vintage slide-dial design), so the mechanism itself is a real gap. Good producibility. Lesson: a strong mechanism idea was dragged down by an unverifiable trend claim used as its main demand justification.

5. **Adaptive Angled Card Holder Rack** — Verified: the accessibility card-holder niche is real (Meeple Like Us exists as a dedicated accessibility review project, and Etsy has an active "arthritis-friendly card holder" sub-market with multiple established listings). Demand capped at 38 rather than higher because this is a genuinely niche audience size, not mass-hobbyist, even though it's a real and gift-driven one. Differentiation capped because the exact space — angled/tiered card racks for arthritis and low-mobility players — is already fairly populated on Etsy with similar framing ("arthritis aid," "little hands," floral card holders), so this reads as a solid execution of an existing idea rather than a new angle. Best producibility and margin in the set: one part, no assembly, one clearly-flagged critical dimension (1.0mm slot width), cheap material, priced at real market comps.

6. **Woodland Faction Meeple Set (Root)** — This is the weakest-supported rationale in the set. My search found Root's actual component reviews describe the tokens as good quality ("linen finish, nice quality" — the reviewer's only complaint was the dice), which directly contradicts the rationale's claim of a "frequently cited complaint" about token quality imbalance between factions. There IS a real existing aftermarket product for this (BGHQ Game Token Upgrade on BGG), which is exactly the problem: that same search proves this is a me-too of an already-existing upgrade set, not an underserved gap, so differentiation is capped hard at 6/15. Demand scored low both for the unverified core claim and the direct-competitor evidence undermining the "gap" framing.

7. **Mars Colony Production Tracker Wheel Set** — The "resource cubes roll off the table" complaint has partial support (a metal-cube Kickstarter add-on exists specifically as an alternative to the stock plastic cubes, implying a recognized issue) but no specific rolling/complaint threads were found, so I scored demand moderately rather than trusting the claim fully. Differentiation is the real problem: search turned up an existing fan-made "Pro" player board that replaces cubes with number-grid tracking for this exact game, meaning the core idea already has a direct competitor in the wild. 16 parts across 8 separate color runs also pushes producibility and margin down — this reads as an epic-scale accessory without an equally epic price justification.

8. **Magnetic Modular Deck Box System** — Demand is well-verified: Pokémon TCG and MTG both posted record confirmed revenue in the most recent fiscal year, and TCG storage is a real spending category. But differentiation is capped hard (6/15) because magnetic modular/expandable 3D-printed deck boxes are already an established, multi-listing Etsy category (e.g., magnetic Commander deck boxes, "Spell Book Deck Box" with magnetic tray) — this idea restates an existing product category rather than adding a new angle. Requiring purchased magnet hardware inserts also dings producibility per the rubric's hardware-insert guidance.

9. **Twist-Lock Poker Chip Carousel Caddy** — Verified: home poker night has a real, currently-documented resurgence (Gen Z-driven revival coverage from multiple sources in 2025/2026), supporting the demand claim better than average. But the rotating lazy-Susan poker chip caddy is not a new product type — vintage and current Etsy listings for "poker chip lazy susan" caddies already exist as an established physical-product category, just not usually 3D-printed — so differentiation is capped. A 200mm rotating disc riding on a printed friction-fit pivot (no bearing) at that diameter is a real wobble/binding risk for a reliable automated-pipeline print, dinging producibility.

10. **Magnetic Snap-Base Miniature Flight Stands** — Demand is the best-verified claim in the whole set: Games Workshop's most recent annual and half-year results show confirmed record revenue and profit growth, and the Etsy magnetic-miniature-base category is extensive and active. However, that same search shows this exact "magnet-ready 3D printed miniature base" concept is already a saturated, multi-seller category on Etsy — this is a well-executed me-too, not a new angle, capping differentiation at 6/15. Strong margin and producibility (cheap material, simple repeated part, magnet-insert tolerances clearly called out).

REQUIREMENT: The tide basin board and all 64 shell-lens discs must exist as 65 discrete, freely liftable, physically separate components. No disc is fused to the board or to any other disc; each disc sits loose in its hollow with a continuous air gap around its full circumference, free to be lifted and flipped by hand.

COMPONENT 1 - Tide basin board. Quantity 1, a single rigid slab 260 x 260 x 18mm overall. The top face carries an 8x8 grid of 64 shallow circular hollows, each 30mm diameter x 3mm deep, arranged on a 32mm center-to-center pitch. Every pair of orthogonally adjacent hollows is separated by a continuous raised dividing ridge standing 2mm proud of the hollow floor, with a 1mm fillet along the ridge's top edge; the ridge network forms a continuous grid spanning the full board so all 64 hollows read as individually bounded depressions rather than a single flat plate. The board is otherwise a single continuous solid; the hollows are subtractive cavities and the ridges are the raised material remaining between them, not separate parts.

COMPONENT 2 - Shell-lens discs. Quantity 64, all identical, each a biconvex-profile puck 28mm diameter x 7mm tall (the edge profile curves outward from a thin rim toward a thicker center on both faces). One face of every disc, the Ridge face, is engraved with exactly 4 concentric rings: each ring 1mm wide x 0.8mm deep, spaced 2mm apart, centered on the disc. The opposite face of the same disc, the Fan face, is engraved with exactly 8 straight radial grooves: each groove 1mm wide x 0.8mm deep, running from the disc's center to its rim, spaced evenly at 45-degree intervals around the disc. Every one of the 64 discs carries both engraved faces on the same physical disc, one motif per side; there is no separate part per face. Each disc seats loose in one 30mm-diameter x 3mm-deep hollow, giving a minimum 1mm radial clearance on every side (15mm hollow radius minus 14mm disc radius = 1mm gap, exceeding the 0.8mm minimum), and stands 4mm proud of the surrounding ridge (7mm disc height minus 3mm hollow depth), enough for a two-finger pinch-and-flip.

REQUIREMENT: The trough board and all 48 seed-weights must exist as 49 discrete, freely liftable, physically separate components. No seed-weight is fused to the board or to any other seed-weight; each sits loose inside a well with a continuous air gap around its full circumference, free to be lifted or poured out by hand.

COMPONENT 1 - Trough board. Quantity 1, a single rigid slab 380 x 170 x 24mm overall, with a rolled bullnose edge (rounded profile, 4mm radius) running the full perimeter of the top edge. Twelve small circular wells are recessed into the top face in two parallel rows of 6, each well 26mm diameter x 16mm deep, with a 2mm rounded fillet at the rim. On the interior wall of every one of the 12 small wells, engrave a single continuous capacity ring: 1mm wide x 0.5mm deep, running horizontally around the full interior circumference of the well at a height 9mm up from the well floor; this ring is a distinct engraved groove line, separate and visually distinguishable from the well's rounded rim fillet at the top opening. Two additional circular store wells are recessed one into each short end of the board: 55mm diameter x 20mm deep each, visibly larger in both diameter and depth than the 12 small wells, with the same 2mm rim fillet but no capacity ring engraved on their interior walls. The board is otherwise a single continuous solid; the 14 wells are subtractive cavities cut into it, not separate parts.

COMPONENT 2 - Seed-weights. Quantity 48 loose cylinders total, all 7mm tall, in 3 diameter groups distinguished only by diameter: 16 small cylinders at 10mm diameter, 16 medium cylinders at 14mm diameter, 16 large cylinders at 18mm diameter. All three groups share the identical 7mm height so that pile height inside a well is a reliable visual gauge against the well's capacity ring regardless of which diameters are mixed in a pile. Each seed-weight is a plain right circular cylinder with a 1mm edge chamfer on both the top and bottom rim. Each seed-weight sits loose inside a well: an 18mm-diameter large seed-weight resting inside a 26mm-diameter small well has a minimum 3mm radial gap between its edge and the well wall on every side (13mm well radius minus 9mm seed radius = 4mm radial clearance), sufficient for two-finger pinch removal; the smaller diameter groups have proportionally greater clearance in the same well.

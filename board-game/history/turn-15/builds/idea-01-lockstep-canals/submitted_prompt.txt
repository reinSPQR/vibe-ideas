REQUIREMENT: The canal board, the loading sill, and all 40 stones (36 lock-stones plus 4 font-stones) must be produced as 42 separate, freely liftable, physically unconnected components. No stone is fused to the board, to the loading sill, or to any other stone. In the exported model each stone sits inside its well or slot with a continuous surrounding air gap of at least 1mm on every side and is not merged, welded, or boolean-unioned with the socket it occupies.

COMPONENT 1 - Canal board. Quantity 1. A single rigid slab measuring 366 x 366 x 15mm overall (footprint including four projecting edge spurs). The top face is milled with a 6x6 grid of 36 square wells, each well 42 x 42mm in plan and 4mm deep, arranged on a 44mm center-to-center pitch, centered on the board. One additional square well, identical in size (42 x 42 x 4mm deep), projects outward from the midpoint of each of the board's four outer edges (4 spur wells total, one per edge). One open-topped rectangular draw bin is recessed into one corner of the board: interior cavity 170 x 90mm in plan x 36mm deep, solid walls and floor, top fully open with no lid, cover, or bridging material spanning the opening, reachable by a hand entering straight down from directly above with nothing overhanging the opening. The board is otherwise a single continuous solid body; the wells and the bin are subtractive cavities cut into it, not separate parts.

COMPONENT 2 - Lock-stones. Quantity 36 loose, individually printed flat square tiles, each 40 x 40 x 8mm. Each stone belongs to one of 4 shape families defined by which of its 4 edges carry a carved trench running from that edge to a shared center hub: Straight family (2 opposite edges grooved, 2 plain edges), Corner family (2 adjacent edges grooved, 2 plain edges), Tee family (3 edges grooved, 1 plain edge), Cross family (all 4 edges grooved, 0 plain edges). Each of the 4 shape families exists in 3 level variants distinguished by tally-notch count: level 1, level 2, level 3. Each shape-and-level combination has exactly 3 identical copies (4 shapes x 3 levels x 3 copies = 36 stones total). On every stone, each grooved edge is cut with a straight trench 5mm wide x 3mm deep with a rounded concave floor, running from that edge inward to a single shared center hub where all of that stone's trenches meet; plain edges carry no trench and are flat. Along the rim of every grooved edge, cut a row of triangular tally notches: the number of notches on every grooved edge of a given stone equals that stone's level (1, 2, or 3); each notch is 2mm wide x 1.5mm deep, and notches are spaced 3mm apart center-to-center along the edge. Every outer edge of every lock-stone (all 4 edges, grooved or plain) carries a 2mm x 45-degree chamfer running the full edge length. Each lock-stone sits loose and unattached inside one 42 x 42 x 4mm board well or one 44 x 44 x 3mm loading-sill slot, with 1mm of plan clearance on each side, and stands proud of the surrounding surface by the difference between the 8mm stone height and the socket depth, free to be lifted straight up by hand.

COMPONENT 3 - Font-stones. Quantity 4, each 40 x 40 x 8mm, same outer envelope and edge chamfer as lock-stones. Each font-stone has exactly one grooved edge, cut with the same 5mm wide x 3mm deep rounded trench as a lock-stone but at level 0: zero tally notches along that edge's rim. That single trench leads from the edge inward to a round scooped reservoir at the tile's center: 8mm diameter x 3mm deep, concave floor. The other 3 edges of every font-stone are plain, with no trench and no notches. Near the corner diagonally opposite the grooved edge, each font-stone carries a row of pierced through-holes, each 3mm diameter, spaced 6mm apart center-to-center: one font-stone has 1 hole, one has 2 holes, one has 3 holes, one has 4 holes, so that all four owner-hole counts (1, 2, 3, 4) are each represented on exactly one font-stone. Font-stones seat loosely in the board's spur wells with the same 1mm-per-side clearance and proud-standing behavior described for lock-stones.

COMPONENT 4 - Loading sill. Quantity 1, a plank 240 x 60 x 12mm. Cut into its top face are 5 square cupped slots arranged in a single row, each 44 x 44mm in plan x 3mm deep, spaced 46mm apart center-to-center. Each of the 5 slots is a distinct depression with its own surrounding lip; the slots do not merge into one continuous trough. Each slot loosely seats one lock-stone or font-stone with the clearances described above.

Model a small tabletop game kit as a single CAD project with the following parts, all in one uniform material.

1. Base board: a flat square plate 120 mm x 120 mm x 6 mm thick, with a 2 mm deep, 1.5 mm wide engraved groove forming a square ring inset 12 mm from every edge. Four through-holes of 6.0 mm diameter, one centred 20 mm in from each corner along both axes.

2. Tokens: 16 separate loose discs, each 18.0 mm diameter and 4.0 mm thick, each with a flat top face carrying an engraved concentric ring 1.0 mm deep and 1.0 mm wide, 4.0 mm in from the disc edge. The 16 discs are separate individual bodies. They are not joined to each other and not joined to the board. Lay them out in a 4 x 4 grid beside the board with a 6 mm gap between adjacent discs.

3. Dial: a disc 40.0 mm diameter and 5.0 mm thick with eight evenly spaced V-notches cut into its rim, each notch 3.0 mm wide and 2.0 mm deep. The dial has a central through-hole 5.2 mm diameter.

4. Dial post: a cylindrical post 5.0 mm diameter and 9.0 mm tall standing on a 24.0 mm diameter, 3.0 mm thick circular foot. The dial sits over the post with a 0.2 mm diametral clearance and must remain a separate body that can rotate freely about the post. The dial is not fused to the post or to the foot.

5. Tray: an open rectangular tray with outer dimensions 70.0 mm x 40.0 mm x 15.0 mm tall, wall thickness 2.5 mm, floor thickness 2.5 mm, open top, square internal corners. It is a separate body from every other part.

Total distinct bodies in the assembly: 1 board + 16 tokens + 1 dial + 1 post-and-foot + 1 tray = 20 separate bodies. No two bodies touch or merge.
